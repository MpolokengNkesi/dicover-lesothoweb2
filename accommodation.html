Before you book

Make sure you check the details of your accommodation before travelling.

Location

Check road access and how far the accommodation is from the places you want to visit.

Price

Ask about meals, transport, deposits and cancellation conditions.

Weather

Pack warm clothing because highland temperatures can change quickly.
