Travel Tips for Exploring Lesotho

Everything you need to know before discovering the Mountain Kingdom.

Lesotho offers different experiences throughout the year. Spring and summer are suitable for green landscapes and outdoor activities, while winter brings cold weather and possible snow in the highlands. Check the forecast before travelling.

Pack comfortable walking shoes, warm clothing, sunscreen, a hat and a waterproof jacket. Temperatures can change quickly, especially in mountainous areas.

Visitors can use taxis, arrange private transport or drive where road conditions allow. For remote mountain destinations, plan your route in advance and check whether a suitable vehicle is needed.

Greet people politely, respect local customs and ask permission before photographing people. Support local businesses and treat cultural sites with care.

Check weather and road conditions before departure. Carry drinking water, a charged phone and essential supplies. Tell someone your route if hiking, and consider a local guide for unfamiliar trails.
